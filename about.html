About Polonnaruwa

History of Polonnaruwa

Polonnaruwa was founded as a major city in the 11th century and became one of the most important capitals of Sri Lanka. It flourished under the rule of kings such as Parakramabahu I, who strengthened the kingdom through military, agricultural, and architectural progress.

Importance of Polonnaruwa

The city played a central role in the political, cultural, and religious life of medieval Sri Lanka. It was admired for its gardens, irrigation systems, religious institutions, and royal architecture, all of which reveal the advanced planning and creativity of the time.

Cultural Significance

Polonnaruwa is deeply connected to Buddhist traditions, artistic performance, and royal patronage. Many of its temples and monuments reflect the religious devotion and cultural identity of the kingdom.

Archaeological Value

The city contains exceptional archaeological remains, including stupas, statues, image houses, ruins of palaces, and large reservoirs. These sites help historians study the development of urban life, religion, and engineering in ancient Sri Lanka.

UNESCO World Heritage

Polonnaruwa is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site because of its outstanding universal value. The remarkable remains of the ancient city demonstrate the creativity, spirituality, and technological achievements of medieval Sri Lankan civilization.

About This Project

This website was created as a 1st Year 1st Semester academic web development project to introduce visitors to the historical heritage, cultural richness, and archaeological value of Polonnaruwa in Sri Lanka.

The aim of this project is to present important historical knowledge in a simple, educational, and visually attractive way using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only.
